Writing to the sound chip by name

The agreed shape: channel, then register, in whole words. Writes that clash with the music driver, the sample channel or the frame interrupt get a warning; the sample channel's own registers wait for section 15.2.

What a NES developer writes

The specification, §9.1 — added under the register block

rasterc today: every line above compiles. The DMC registers, apu.dmc.* at $4010 to $4013, are intended and not built: sample playback steals CPU cycles at unpredictable times, and how that meets a timed block is still open (section 15.2). Reach them through poke until then.

The specification, §9.5 — the opening sentence, and four new rows' worth of reasons

Every named register is in the table below, with whether it reads and whether it accepts writes. ppu.status is the only one that can be read and not written. apu.status ($4015) is the one sound register that reads back: a read gives the length-counter and interrupt flags, and clears the frame-interrupt flag. apu.frame_counter is written at $4017, and a read of $4017 is refused because it reads controller 2.

The cost: the reader loses the at-a-glance totals; the sentence never goes stale.
